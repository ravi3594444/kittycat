# 🐈‍⬛ Freedom Fusion AI LIVE Studio — Complete Product Vision

The final product will be an AI-powered TikTok LIVE studio that you control from your iPhone using voice commands. Your computer runs the animated AI cat, while you walk around Manhattan, join the LIVE, talk with viewers, and let the cat interact with the audience.

> *Note: This is the planned product vision, not a list of features already implemented.*

## 3. How You Actually Use the Finished Product

1. **Start with your voice:**
   > *"ChatGPT, start Freedom Fusion LIVE. Have Whiskers talk about cybersecurity. I'll join shortly."*
   > The remote controller launches the streaming services and attempts the verified TikTok LIVE startup procedure.
2. **Receive your notification:**
   Pushcut notifies you when TikTok confirms the broadcast is LIVE. Tap to open the stream using your personal TikTok account and request to join as a guest.
3. **Walk, speak, and interact:**
   > *"Hey Whiskers, tell everyone how cellular handoffs work in Manhattan."*
   > The cat recognizes your authorized command through the guest-audio pipeline (once integrated), or you can dictate commands directly into TikTok comments.
4. **Let Whiskers handle the audience:**
   The cat answers relevant comments, follows the current topic, and uses approved knowledge from Notion while prioritizing your commands.
5. **Finish and save everything:**
   Tell Whiskers to wrap up. The system summarizes the LIVE, saves approved notes, records useful questions, and prepares suggested video clips.

## 4. Release Roadmap (How Much to Build First)

Rather than building all 40+ capabilities at once, build in three phases:

| Phase | Priority | Core Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **V1 — Working AI LIVE Cat** | **Build First** | Remote startup, TikTok comment ingestion, animated speaking cat, ChatGPT answers, iPhone notification, and authorized text commands. |
| **V2 — Humanized Conversations** | **Next** | Guest voice recognition, wake phrases, interruption handling, viewer recognition, and Notion knowledge retrieval. |
| **V3 — Intelligent Creator Studio** | **Later** | Long-term memory, 10-minute voice briefings, audience analytics, saved recordings, clip suggestions, and multi-session learning. |

---

## 5. What Still Needs Proof Before Development?

* **TikTok Desktop & Guest Workflow:** Three TikTok-dependent steps are unconfirmed: starting an actual LIVE automatically through the desktop interface, joining that desktop-hosted broadcast as a video guest, and capturing guest audio cleanly for the cat.
* **Connector Compliance:** The comment connector is unofficial, so reliability and platform compliance must be evaluated. TikTok's native guest layout and LIVE rules cannot be bypassed by a custom dashboard.

### Summary Recommendation
The finished product should feel like having your own **AI production assistant and AI co-host**, not operating a streaming studio. You say *"Go live"*, join from your iPhone, speak naturally, and Whiskers handles the conversation and knowledge retrieval. **The V1 goal is to prove that core experience with minimal taps.**
